Privacy Policy

Bubble Snake Crash · Last updated: 21 September 2026

The short version. Bubble Snake Crash collects a nickname you choose and the scores you post, so the leaderboard can work. No email, no real name, no location, no contacts, no advertising and no tracking. You can delete all of it yourself, from inside the game, at any time.

Who we are

Bubble Snake Crash is published by BERYLG, SIA, a company registered in Latvia (registration no. 42103099442). We are the controller of the data described below. Contact: danucdstudio@gmail.com.

What we collect

DataWhy
An anonymous account ID, created automatically when you first open the game So your scores and nickname stay linked to you. It is a random code and is not connected to your name, email or phone.
The nickname you choose Shown publicly on the leaderboard next to your scores.
Your scores: points, number of moves, time taken, game mode and when you played To rank players on the leaderboard and check that scores are genuine.

That is everything we store on our servers. Your nickname and scores are public — anyone playing Bubble Snake Crash can see them on the leaderboard. Please don't use your real name, or anything else that identifies you, as a nickname.

What stays on your device only

Your settings (sound, vibration, keypad), which skins you own and have equipped, your bubble balance, your personal best scores and your daily goals are stored on your phone and are never sent to us.

What we don't collect

No email address, real name, phone number, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers for advertising, or browsing activity. The game contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see or store your payment details. Those companies handle your purchase under their own privacy policies.

Why we're allowed to use it

We process your account ID, nickname and scores because they are needed to provide the leaderboard feature you use (performance of a contract, GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)), and to keep the leaderboard fair by detecting fake scores and blocking offensive names (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f)).

Where it's stored

Leaderboard data is stored by our hosting provider, Supabase, on servers in Frankfurt, Germany, inside the European Union. Supabase processes it only on our instructions.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, your nickname and every score you posted are removed permanently and immediately.

Your rights

Delete everything yourself: in the game, go to Settings → Account → Delete account. This removes your account, nickname and all your scores from our servers.

You can also ask us to show you, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it, by writing to danucdstudio@gmail.com. If you believe we have mishandled your data, you can complain to the Latvian Data State Inspectorate (Datu valsts inspekcija) or the data protection authority in your country.

Children

Bubble Snake Crash is a game for everyone and asks for no personal information beyond a nickname. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children beyond that. If you are a parent and believe your child has used a nickname that identifies them, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
