Privacy Policy

Bubble Snake Crash · Last updated: 28 September 2026

The short version. Bubble Snake Crash stores a nickname you choose and the scores you post, so the leaderboard can work. There are no ads in the game. We never ask for your email, real name, location or contacts, and you can delete your account from inside the game at any time.

Who we are

Bubble Snake Crash is published by BERYLG, SIA, a company registered in Latvia (registration no. 42103099442). We are the controller of the leaderboard data described below. Contact: danucdstudio@gmail.com.

What we collect

DataWhy
An anonymous account ID, created automatically when you first open the game So your scores and nickname stay linked to you. It is a random code and is not connected to your name, email or phone.
The nickname you choose Shown publicly on the leaderboard next to your scores.
Your scores: points, number of moves, time taken, game mode and when you played To rank players on the leaderboard and check that scores are genuine.

Your nickname and scores are public — anyone playing Bubble Snake Crash can see them on the leaderboard. Please don't use your real name, or anything else that identifies you, as a nickname.

Advertising

The current version of Bubble Snake Crash shows no ads and contains no advertising code, so no data is collected for advertising.

If a future version adds ads, this policy will be updated before that version is released, explaining what the ad provider collects and how you can say no. Where the law requires your consent — for example for personalised ads in the EU, EEA and UK — the game will ask for it first.

What stays on your device only

Your settings (sound, vibration, direction pad), which skins you own and have equipped, your bubble balance and your daily goals are stored on your phone only and are never sent to us. Your phone also keeps its own copy of your personal bests; the scores themselves reach us only as described above, when they are posted to the leaderboard.

What we don't collect

We never ask for or store your email address, real name, phone number, location, contacts or photos. We don't use any analytics or tracking tools of our own.

Purchases

If the game offers purchases with real money, they are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see or store your payment details.

Why we're allowed to use it

Leaderboard data is used because it's needed to provide the leaderboard you use (performance of a contract, GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)), and to keep it fair by detecting fake scores and blocking offensive names (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f)).

Where it's stored

Leaderboard data is stored by our hosting provider, Supabase, on servers in Frankfurt, Germany, inside the European Union. Supabase processes it only on our instructions.

How long we keep it

Leaderboard data is kept for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, your nickname and every score you posted are removed permanently and immediately.

Your rights

Delete everything yourself: in the game, go to Settings → Account → Delete account. This removes your account, nickname and all your scores from our servers.

You can also ask us to show you, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it, by writing to danucdstudio@gmail.com. If you believe we have mishandled your data, you can complain to the Latvian Data State Inspectorate (Datu valsts inspekcija) or the data protection authority in your country.

Children

Bubble Snake Crash is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children. If you are a parent and believe your child has used a nickname that identifies them, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

See also the Terms of Use. © BERYLG, SIA
